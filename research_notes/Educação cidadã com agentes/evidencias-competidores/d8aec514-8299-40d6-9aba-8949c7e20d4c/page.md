What is Khanmigo? How does AI tutoring work?
Khanmigo is an AI-powered personal tutor from trusted education nonprofit Khan Academy. For learners and families, Khanmigo offers engaging and on-topic tutoring and is ethically designed with safety and learning as a top priority. Khanmigo is the only AI tool that is incorporated into Khan Academy’s world-class content library, which covers math, humanities, coding, social studies, and more. You can get personalized tutoring on every exercise, video, and article on Khan Academy. Unlike other AI tools such as ChatGPT, Khanmigo doesn’t just give answers. Instead, with limitless patience, it guides learners to find the answer themselves. When rating AI tools for learning, Common Sense Media gave Khanmigo 4 stars, rising above other AI tools like ChatGPT and Bard.
What can an AI study buddy do? What subjects can Khanmigo help with?
Khanmigo can help you master skills with personalized tutoring on Khan Academy's world-class content. Like any good tutor, Khanmigo doesn't just tell you the answer. Instead, it gently guides you to find the answers yourself. Whether you’re studying for an exam or just want to learn something new, Khanmigo can help you with almost any academic subject, including math, science, coding, history, and humanities. 
How do I get Khanmigo? Can anyone use it?
To access Khanmigo as a parent or learner, you must make a monthly or annual payment, be 18 years or older, live in the United States, and have a billing address in the U.S.
If you are under 18 years old, your parent or guardian needs to sign up in order for you to gain access. Please ask your parent or guardian to sign up and add you to their account.
Is Khanmigo an AI tool that can help me with writing, coding, and job seeking?

No, you will not be able to grant students access to Khanmigo. At this time, classroom access for Khanmigo is only available through school or district implementations. We invite district and school administrators to contact our Districts Partnership team to learn about Khanmigo for Districts. 
Khanmigo for teachers offers a lot of exciting features, such as standards-aligned lesson planning tied to Khan Academy’s world-class content library; an on-demand summary of recent student work so you can quickly assess progress and identify areas where additional support is needed; and Khanmigo-crafted learning objectives, rubrics, and even exit tickets.
As a nonprofit educational organization, we see it as our responsibility to explore what AI could mean for the future of education.
